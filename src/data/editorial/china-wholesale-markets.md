# China Wholesale Markets: Where to Go by Product Category

**Short answer.** China's wholesale markets are clustered by product. Small commodities, gifts and stationery point to Yiwu. Apparel, shoes, bags, toys and cosmetics point to Guangzhou. Electronic components point to Shenzhen's Huaqiangbei. Fabric by the metre points to Keqiao, and furniture to Foshan and Shunde. A market is a good place to compare ready-made products, price ranges and samples. It does not prove who makes a product or whether the next batch will match the sample.

*Desk-checked on 9 November 2026 against official market notices, government releases, fair organisers and published buyer guides, with added notes from local trade knowledge. Opening hours, floor layouts and house rules change, and some sources disagree. Where they do, this page says so. Confirm details on the day.*

## What a wholesale market can and cannot tell you

A wholesale market is one of four places buyers look for suppliers. Each answers a different question.

| Type | Good for | Limits | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wholesale market | Seeing ready-made products, comparing price ranges, taking samples | A stall may be a trader, a wholesaler or a factory sales window | Ask who produces the item and where |
| Factory | Production capacity, process, quality control | May not show products the way a market stall does | Visit with a checklist |
| Industrial cluster | Learning where a product category is made | A cluster is a region, not a recommended supplier | Narrow to specific companies |
| Online platform | First screening and contact | Listings need independent verification | Samples, video calls, visits or document checks |

### Who is behind the stall?

A "factory direct" sign is not evidence. Yiwu's official market operator says its online shops and offline booths correspond one to one, which says nothing about whether each booth is a manufacturer. Ask:

- Does the seller make this product, buy it in, or both?
- Can they state the producing company and its address?
- Is the item ready stock, or made to order?
- What are the unit, the minimum order and the packing for the quoted price?
- Does the price include printing, packaging, tax and delivery?

Record the answers exactly as given. They are leads to check later, not proof.

## Market profiles

### Yiwu

**Best for:** small commodities, gifts, jewellery, stationery, hardware, socks, hats and many other low-cost categories.

**What to know**

- The operator's market page lists Districts 1 to 4 as open 08:30 to 17:30, with entrances closing at 16:30 (17:00 in District 3). District 5 closes at 17:00. [Chinagoods markets page](https://m.chinagoods.com/market/) The operator's own FAQ says opening arrangements follow notices posted on site.
- Older notices and other sources give different closing times for some markets. Hours for the Huangyuan garment market range from 07:30 to 20:30 down to 17:00, and the International Production Materials Market is listed as closing at 15:30 in one place and 17:30 in others. Check before you go.
- Individual stalls may open later or close earlier than the building.
- Addresses are usually given as district, gate, floor, street and booth number. Write all five down.
- The operator lists a free guided-visit service and a navigation tool that finds booths by name or number. The guide service requires advance booking.
- Buyer guides describe a free shuttle from Yiwu railway station and a suburban rail stop named International Trade City. Check current routes on a map app.
- No market-wide photography rule was found. Each vendor decides. Some refuse, some charge, and some welcome photos and livestreams. Always ask before photographing a stall or product.

**Plan the first visit.** Pick one or two districts that match your product and stay there. The operator says a three-minute stop at every booth would take more than a year of eight-hour days. Do not try to cover the whole market in a day.

**Where sources disagree.** Floor-level details in the table above, closing times for some markets, and what is now on District 5's top floor.

**Beyond the market.** The Trade City is a place to review ready-made products and take samples. For custom work, repeat orders or a quality process you need to see, ask who produces the item and move to a verified supplier. See [Yiwu sourcing and supplier visits](/destinations/yiwu/).



### Guangzhou

Guangzhou is not one market. Apparel, shoes, bags, fabric, toys, stationery and cosmetics sit in different districts. Treat each as its own trip.

|---|---|---|

| Shahe area | Budget volume womenswear | Wholesale-led; some stalls open very early. Sources recommend finishing before noon. |
| Baima and nearby malls | Mid-to-high-end apparel | Mixed wholesale and retail; second-tier pricing. The Baima site lists 08:00 to 18:00 and the Guangzhou Railway Station metro stop. [Baima](https://www.baima.com/contact.html) |
| Zhanxi Road | Finished shoes | Near the railway station. Guangda Shoes City is reported at 901 Qingcha Road in Shijing, Baiyun, not on Zhanxi Road. |
| Sanyuanli and Ziyuangang | Leather bags | Baiyun World Leather Trading Center is on Jiefang North Road. Sources describe a mix of wholesale, retail and tourism. |
| Zhongda textile area | Trims and fabric | Guangzhou International Textile City describes about 57 specialist markets in the Zhongda cluster. [GZITN](https://wap.gzitn.com/news_content.php?newsid=4072) Sources list trims in the basement. |

**Beyond the market.** Many Guangzhou stalls are sales points for suppliers in the wider Pearl River Delta. See [Guangzhou and Dongguan supplier visits](/destinations/guangzhou-dongguan/).



#### Toys, gifts and party goods: Yide Road

The Yide Road area in Yuexiu district is Guangzhou's main toy, stationery and gift cluster. Published listings name Onelink Plaza (also written Wanling Plaza) at 39 Jiefang South Road, the International Toys and Stationery Boutique Plaza at 390 to 426 Yide West Road, Zhonggang Toys City, Debao and Yijingyuan. Nearest metro: Haizhu Square (Lines 2 and 6) or Yide Road station on Line 6, depending on the mall. Exits are reported differently in different guides, so check the exit on the day.

**Onelink Plaza floors.** Published listings describe plush and educational toys in the basement, boutique goods and accessories on the lower floors, stationery, gifts and packaging on floors 3 and 4, home gifts, crafts and festival goods on floors 5 and 6, and manufacturer showrooms and foreign-trade orders on floors 7 to 9. Local trade knowledge adds that export-oriented styles are concentrated on floors 1 to 3, and that foreign-trade order desks and manufacturer showrooms are mixed in with other tenants on the upper floors. If time is short, concentrate on the basement and floors 1 to 3. Hours are listed as 09:00 to 18:00, and local trade knowledge says stalls are normally open all day.

**The other malls.** The International Toys and Stationery Boutique Plaza and nearby malls are described as mainly affordable toys, basic student stationery, festival goods and bubble toys, with lower minimums. Yijingyuan is described as small accessories and gifts and can be skipped when time is short.

**What it does not prove.** Guangzhou has relatively few toy factories, and much of the toy stock in these malls is understood to come from other production areas, notably Chenghai in Shantou, Yiwu and Dongguan, with stalls acting as distribution points. Ask where each item is made. Toys need test reports for the market you sell in.



#### Stationery and art supplies: Huangsha

Huangsha in Liwan district is Guangzhou's best-known stationery and art-supplies wholesale area. Two names recur in published listings:

- **Yiyuan Office and Art Supplies Trading Center**, 25 Huangsha Hou Road. A consumer guide describes student stationery and sports goods on the ground floor and art supplies and craft materials on the second, with stalls that tend to specialise.
- **Xingzhiguang Cultural and Sports Market**, with a Huangsha branch at 28 to 40 Huangsha Hou Road and a second branch on Nan'an Road. A district listing calls it a first-tier wholesale hub for stationery and sports goods in Guangdong.

A district list gives the walk from Huangsha metro station exit C to Yiyuan as about 250 metres. [Liwan district list](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20240614A09X1400) Consumer guides give opening hours of 09:00 to 18:00 for Yiyuan and 10:00 to 18:00 for the Xingzhiguang Huangsha south hall, but these come from a 2023 article and should be checked.

**What it does not prove.** That a stall makes what it sells. Many stalls here are wholesalers or brand distributors, so ask who produces the item. Local trade knowledge says the Nan'an Road area suits large-volume buying, but published listings describe first-tier wholesale rather than factory direct. Large brand stationery is understood to be produced mostly outside Guangzhou, with only small-scale production in Panyu and Baiyun.



#### Cosmetics: stalls and factories are different trips

Finished-goods wholesale and contract manufacturing sit in different places.

- **Wholesale malls** for ready stock cluster around Guangyuan West Road, Xingfa North Road and Airport Road in Baiyun and Yuexiu. Xingfa Plaza, Yifa Plaza, Meiwan Plaza and Dingfa Plaza adjoin each other, and the cluster can be reached from Feixiang Park station on Line 2. Meibo City (121 Guangyuan West Road, near Sanyuanli station on Line 2) is the stop for professional salon equipment, beauty devices, and hair and nail products. Published addresses differ between guides because the malls sit along several streets.
- **Hours.** Yifa Plaza is listed at 08:00 to 18:00 and Dingfa Plaza at 09:00 to 20:00. Local trade knowledge says stalls in this cluster are open from 08:00 to 18:00 and do not close early. Hours vary by stall.
- **Contract manufacturers** (OEM and ODM) are in factory districts, not in the malls. Industry articles name Baiyun (Renhe, Taihe and Zhongluotan) and Huadu as the main areas. These articles are written by manufacturers and service providers, so treat them as pointers, not rankings.
- **Licence check.** Guangdong's cosmetics safety regulation requires cosmetics manufacturers to hold a production licence and to produce within its scope. [Guangzhou market regulator](https://scjgj.gz.gov.cn/ztzl/ygaflfgzczl/lpyxl/gd/content/post_9144191.html) Ask to see the licence and check that its scope covers your product type.
- **Destination rules.** Requirements depend on where you will sell. Ask which market's rules the factory has worked to and ask for the documents you need.
- **Counterfeits.** Do not buy imitation branded products. Customs seizure risk is high.



#### A one-day Guangzhou plan for toys, gifts and cosmetics (example)

This is an example, not a fixed itinerary. It assumes you travel by metro. Parking and traffic in both areas are hard to predict, so metro is simpler than driving.

**Morning: Yide Road (toys, stationery, gifts, party goods)**

1. Take the metro to Haizhu Square (Lines 2 and 6) or Yide Road (Line 6).
2. Start at Onelink Plaza. Cover the basement and floors 1 to 3 first, where plush toys, trend toys and export-oriented gifts and festival goods are concentrated. Add floors 3 and 4 for stationery, gifts and packaging if you have time. Skip the home gifts on the higher floors if the schedule is tight.
3. Walk to the International Toys and Stationery Boutique Plaza and Debao for affordable toys, basic student stationery and festival goods.
4. Add Yijingyuan only if time allows.
5. Collect business cards and samples in the morning rather than placing orders on the spot.

**What to ask**

- Unit price, minimum order, and whether a full container is possible.
- Sample price, and whether the sample fee is credited against a later order.
- Test reports and certificates for your destination market.
- Whether photographs are allowed. Export-only styles often are not, so ask first.
- Whether the quote includes tax and packing, and the lead time.
- Payment terms.

**Cautions.** Quotes often exclude freight and customs clearance. Many toys are sold by the dozen or by the carton, not singly.

**Lunch.** Eat near the mall and leave around 12:40.

**Transfer.** Take Line 2 from Haizhu Square towards Jiahewanggang and get off at Feixiang Park. Local trade knowledge gives exit A and a walk of about 300 metres to the Xingfa cluster.

**Afternoon: Guangyuan West Road, Xingfa and Airport Road (cosmetics)**

1. Visit Xingfa, Yifa, Meiwan and Dingfa, which adjoin each other. Local trade knowledge says the ground floors hold colour cosmetics, popular hair and body care, packaging and raw-material traders, with the most overseas customers. Floors 2 and 3 hold skincare, mask sets, and OEM and ODM service providers who can connect you to factories.
2. Add Meibo City only if you need professional salon equipment, beauty devices, or hair and nail products. For ready-stock colour cosmetics and skincare, the Xingfa and Yifa cluster is enough.

**What to ask**

- Ready-stock unit price.
- For private label: sampling time and minimum order.
- Domestic registration documents, and the export documents your market needs.
- Whether they can provide compliance documents for other countries.
- Lead time, packing options and payment terms.

**Cautions.** Do not buy imitation branded products. Separate ready-stock distributors from private-label and contract-manufacturing intermediaries. Ready stock moves quickly, while private label usually has much higher minimums.

**Late afternoon.** Sort your cards and samples. Ask whether the mall has courier counters, so you can post samples instead of carrying them.

**Optional second day**

- **Huangsha:** stationery and art supplies, mainly brand distributors and wholesalers.
- **Baiyun and Huadu factory districts:** for contract manufacturing. Visit factories, not stalls, and arrange the visits in advance.

**General cautions for foreign buyers**

- Collect information from several suppliers and compare prices and credentials after you return. Avoid large orders on a first visit.
- Confirm certificates and export documents early, because rules differ by country.
- Ask for a structure where part is paid up front and the balance after inspection, and avoid full prepayment to sellers you do not know.
- Arrange sample shipping separately and agree who pays the freight.



### Shenzhen: Huaqiangbei

Huaqiangbei is not one building. It is a district of many specialist malls, around 1.45 square kilometres by one guide's count.

- **SEG Electronics Market** (SEG Plaza, 1002 Huaqiang North Road) and **Huaqiang Electronics World** focus on components, modules and boards.
- Phone and accessory trading is spread over several other malls.
- **Mingtong Digital City** has been reported to have converted from phone accessories to imported beauty products. Some English-language guides still describe it as a phone accessories market. Do not plan a route from older guides.
- **Metro:** Huaqiangbei station is on Lines 2 and 7. Line 1's Huaqianglu station is a different station at the district's southern end.
- **Hours:** Tourist-oriented guides say most malls open around 09:00 to 10:00 and close around 18:00 to 19:00. Wholesale-oriented guides say many counters are busiest after midday, and phone quotes are often updated in the afternoon. This page does not give a fixed time.
- **Photography:** The second-hand phone market known as Tongtiandi (formerly Feiyang) is reported to prohibit photography. Elsewhere, ask first.

**What Huaqiangbei is good for.** Buying and testing ready-stock components and accessories in small quantities, finding substitute parts, and checking prototypes.

**What it is not.** A place to find a factory by walking in. Published guides place mass production and development in Bantian, Longgang, Guangming, Pingshan, Nanshan and Shekou, and in Dongguan. See [Shenzhen electronics sourcing](/destinations/shenzhen/).



### Keqiao (Shaoxing)

Keqiao hosts China Textile City, the largest concentration of dedicated fabric markets in China.

- The operator lists seven specialist textile markets: Dongsheng Road, East, Lianhe, North, Beilian, Tianhui and Grey Cloth. It cites about 1.49 million square metres of building area, more than 20,000 business units, more than 50,000 fabric types, and exports to over 190 countries and regions. [China Textile City group](https://www.qfcgroup.com/news/index_13_15.html?page=8)
- **The North Market's published hours are 09:00 to 17:10** on the operator's online platform, which also lists more than 4,400 business units there. [North Market](https://www.qfc.cn/marketintro/1.html) Hours for other markets come from map listings and need checking.
- The **International Fabric Sourcing Center** (a rebuilt former West Market) is a five-storey stall-style fabric market. [Keqiao government notice](http://wz.kq.gov.cn/art/2019/6/18/art_1613601_37081884.html) The **International Trade Zone** is a separate, office-style district for company-level trading. [Keqiao government page](https://www.kq.gov.cn/art/2018/10/30/art_1562162_22697512.html)
- Booth addresses in official listings usually read as market, zone, floor and room number.
- **Getting there.** One fair visitor guide gives about 9.8 km (20 minutes) from Shaoxing North station, about 27 km (55 minutes) from Hangzhou Xiaoshan airport and about 44 km (80 minutes) from Hangzhou East station. Rail times from Yiwu to Shaoxing North are reported between roughly 1 hour 7 minutes and 1 hour 34 minutes, so check the train timetable.
- **Photography:** Some stalls allow it and some do not. Ask.

**Record the specification, not the impression.** Fabric listings often use different units and definitions for the same field.

| Field | What to write down | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | Fibre and percentage | Wording varies between listings |
| Width | Number and unit, and whether it is overall width or usable width | The same page may show several widths |
| Weight | Number and unit, grams per square metre or grams per metre | The two systems give very different numbers |
| Yarn count, density, weave or knit gauge | Full figures | Needed to compare like with like |
| Colour reference | Pantone code or sample number | Screens and lighting distort colour |
| Dye lot | Lot number, minimum per colour, small-lot fee | Different lots can differ in shade |
| Minimum order | Quantity and unit: metres, kilograms or rolls | Units differ between suppliers |
| Sampling | Free or paid, lead time | Strike-off and small-lot fees may be extra |
| Stock or made to order | Lead time and deposit | Deposits are often required to start production |
| Price terms | Tax, delivery, invoice and validity | Some listings state prices exclude tax and freight |

**Beyond the market.** Many shops are traders. If you need consistent supply, ask who weaves and dyes, then verify the mill. See [Keqiao fabric market visits](/destinations/shaoxing-keqiao/).



### Foshan and Shunde: furniture

Two nearby towns play different roles.

| | Lecong | Longjiang |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Trading, showrooms, dealers | Manufacturing and materials |
| Reported scale | About 3 million square metres of showroom space and more than 3,450 dealers (media figures) | More than 2,300 furniture manufacturers (media figures; other sources give 2,800) and ten large materials markets |
| Good for | Choosing styles, comparing finishes, meeting dealers | Seeing materials markets and manufacturer showrooms |

- Louvre Furniture Center lists daily hours of 09:00 to 18:30. [Louvre](https://louvre.cn/news/group/1668.html) The company also has a separate branch in Guangzhou, so check the address.
- Louvre's site gives drive times of about 30 minutes to Foshan West station, 45 minutes to Guangzhou South station and 60 minutes to Baiyun airport, plus a Guangfo metro route that changes to a bus at Kuiqi Road station. [Louvre transport](https://louvre.cn/tourist-attractions/walk) Reported train times from Guangzhou South to Shunde range from 8 to 16 minutes, depending on which station is named.
- Retail and wholesale mix varies by mall and by exhibitor. Sources do not agree on every mall.
- The Longjiang International Furniture Fair has strict visitor rules in organisers' notices: pre-registration, no photography or filming, and no admission for under-16s. The August 2026 edition used six venues linked by free shuttles.
- CIFF Guangzhou's 59th edition is scheduled for 18 to 21 March and 28 to 31 March 2027. [CIFF](https://ciff-gz.com/en) The 2027 Longjiang fair dates were not available when this page was checked.

## Market names that trip people up

- **Binwang Market in Yiwu** was described in a 2024 municipal government reply as moved out in 2011 and redeveloped as the Binwang 158 creative park. [Yiwu government](https://www.yw.gov.cn/art/2024/6/14/art_1229886788_4241880.html) Do not list it as an active garment or knitwear wholesale market.
- **Mingtong Digital City in Shenzhen** is reported to have changed use, as above.
- **Guangda Shoes City** is reported to be in Shijing, Baiyun, not at the Zhanxi Road shoe district.
- **Yide Road is not the cosmetics district.** Finished cosmetics are concentrated around Guangyuan West Road and Xingfa.
- **North Market, Beilian Market, East Gate cotton market and the Grey Cloth Market** in Keqiao are separate markets with different addresses.
- **The International Fabric Sourcing Center is not the International Trade Zone.**
- **Lecong and Longjiang** are different towns with different jobs.
- **Louvre in Foshan is not Louvre in Guangzhou.**
- **Huaqiangbei station and Huaqianglu station** are different metro stops.
- **Xintang and Jun'an** are denim clusters outside the city centres, not central wholesale markets.

Verify the market name and address before you plan a route.

## How to visit a wholesale market

### Before you go

- Product, use and target market.
- A reference image or physical sample.
- A quantity range, not a single number.
- Packaging and branding requirements.
- The list of fields you must record for this product (for fabric, use the table above).
- A sample budget and a plan for carrying or shipping.
- A single enquiry template to send to every candidate later.
- The market's own holiday and opening notices for your dates.

### What to record at every stall

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Sample ID | Use one scheme, for example date, market code and a running number |
| Market, district, gate, floor, street, booth | Without all of these you cannot find the stall again |
| Contact and card photo | Match to the sample ID |
| Product name and model | Identical-looking products can have different model numbers |
| Material and specification | Dimensions, weight, material, voltage or certification as relevant |
| Price, currency and price terms | Say whether it includes tax, packing, printing and delivery |
| Unit and minimum order | In reported cases "one piece" meant a carton of hundreds of items; ask |
| Packing | Per carton, per pack, per roll |
| Sample status | Ready stock or made to order, fee, refundable or not |
| Producer stated | The company name and address the seller gave, written word for word |
| Quote validity | Quotes given verbally may not hold |
| Photo IDs and next action | Revisit, send enquiry, move to factory or drop |

### Sample control

- One sample, one record, one set of photos.
- Never put samples from several stalls in one bag.
- Label every sample and keep a separate sheet that links labels to stalls.
- If photography is refused, write the specification down by hand.
- Check carrier rules before shipping batteries, liquids or fragile items home.
- Within a day of returning, match records, photos, cards and samples, then send everyone the same specification.

### Photos and permission

No market-wide photography rule was found for Yiwu, and rules differ by vendor and market. Some operators or fairs prohibit it outright. Ask before photographing any stall, product or price tag.

## From market sample to verified supplier

### When the market is enough

- You are buying standard ready stock with no changes.
- The order is small and one-off.
- You mainly want to compare styles and price ranges.
- You have no special requirements for production, process or quality.

### When to look behind the stall

- You need customisation: size, material, logo, packaging or colour.
- You expect repeat orders.
- Materials, process or test requirements are specific.
- Two quotes differ and the specification does not explain it.
- The seller cannot say who makes the product or where.
- You need to see the production line or quality process.
- Certification or destination-market compliance applies.

### A four-step workflow

1. Choose two or three candidates from your market samples.
2. Ask each for the producing company and its address and record the answer exactly.
3. Send every candidate the same specification, covering quantity tiers, packing, tax, delivery time and quote validity.
4. Decide to continue online, visit, or drop the supplier.

Useful next pages: [verify the manufacturer](/guides/factory-vs-trading-company-china/), [factory visit checklist](/guides/china-factory-visit-checklist/) and [compare suppliers with the same evidence](/tools/factory-visit-scorecard/).

## Online platforms vs wholesale markets

| Point | Physical market | Online platform |
|---|---|---|
| Seeing the product | You can touch, test and compare side by side | Depends on photos, video and samples |
| Range | Limited to the market you visit | Wider, with varying quality of information |
| Negotiation | Face to face | Text or video; details can get lost |
| Best stage | Finding styles, learning price ranges, taking samples | Screening, contact and repeat enquiries |
| Main risk | Stall identity is unclear | A "verified" label does not mean producer |

Check the platform's current rules and fees yourself. They change.

## Planning your trip

### Scale

Yiwu is big enough that you need a plan. The operator cites 8 million square metres and about 2.1 million products. [Chinagoods guide](https://www.chinagoods.com/information/21/20261023168400) In Keqiao, one of the markets alone has more than 4,000 units. Pick your product first, then the market, then the district.

### Example routes (examples, not fixed itineraries)

| Route | Days | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Zhejiang | 2 to 3 | Day 1: one or two Yiwu districts matching your product. Day 2: train to Keqiao for fabric, with time to log swatches. Day 3: spare day to revisit or move to a factory |
| Guangzhou toys, gifts and cosmetics | 1 to 2 | Morning at Yide Road, afternoon at the Xingfa cosmetics cluster, optional second day at Huangsha or at cosmetics factories (see the Guangzhou plan above) |
| Pearl River Delta | 3 | Day 1: Guangzhou in the morning, then another Guangzhou district. Day 2: morning intercity train to Shenzhen and Huaqiangbei in the afternoon. Day 3: add Foshan and Shunde if furniture is on the list |
| Around a fair | 2 to 3 | Leave market days before and after the fair, and keep fair days for the fair |

The order of Guangzhou and Shenzhen follows how the markets work: Guangzhou apparel trades mainly in the morning, and many Huaqiangbei counters are busiest after midday. Intercity trains between Guangzhou East and Shenzhen are reported at roughly an hour (published figures range from 57 to 68 minutes). The Guangzhou South to Shenzhen North high-speed link can take as little as 29 minutes, but both stations are away from the market districts.

## FAQ

**Are wholesale markets in China open to small buyers?**
Often yes, but it is decided stall by stall. Many Yiwu stalls decline retail. Shisanhang and Shahe stalls mostly decline retail, while malls around Baima mix both. District 6 in Yiwu is reported to support retail. Ask every stall what it will sell and at what price.

**Can I buy directly from factories in a wholesale market?**
Sometimes. A stall may be a trader, a wholesaler or a factory sales window. A "factory direct" sign is not proof. Check the business licence scope, the production address, whether they can make samples, and whether you can see production.

**Is a China wholesale market cheaper than Alibaba or 1688?**
You cannot compare them directly. Booth prices may exclude printing, packaging, tax and shipping, and online prices may assume different minimum orders. Compare landed cost for the same specification.

**How many markets can I visit in one day?**
There is no fixed number. It depends on the product, how many samples you carry, how long each discussion takes and the market's hours. Yiwu is too large for a quick sweep, and Guangzhou apparel mostly trades in the morning. The Guangzhou plan above covers two areas in one day, which works because both are compact clusters.

**Do I need a business licence to buy at a market?**
No single rule covering every market was found. Arrangements depend on the market and the stall, and may differ for invoices and exports. Ask the stall and check the market's notices.

**Can I order samples after I go home?**
Often through the contact details you collected, but it depends on the seller. Agree the sample fee, shipping cost and any refund terms in writing, and check carrier rules for batteries, liquids and fragile items.

**Should I visit a market or a factory first?**
Start with a market to see styles and price ranges. Go to the factory when you need custom work, repeat supply, or proof of capacity and quality. If you already have a shortlist, you can go straight to a factory.
