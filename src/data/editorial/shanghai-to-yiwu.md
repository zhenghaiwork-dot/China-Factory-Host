# Shanghai to Yiwu: Travel Options and Arrival Planning

The simplest route for most visitors is a direct high-speed train from Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station to Yiwu, followed by a local transfer to your hotel or market district. Many direct services take roughly 1.5–2 hours; the fastest listed services are around 1 hour 20 minutes. Check the duration of the train you actually book—not just the fastest journey advertised.

Public transport sources reviewed on 12 November 2026. Journey ranges and connection buffers are planning estimates, not timed field measurements. Confirm your train, local transfers and luggage requirements with the operators before travelling.

The train ride is only part of the trip. You also need to reach Hongqiao, pass security, find your boarding gate and travel from Yiwu station to your exact destination.

For a first visit, arrive at Hongqiao Railway Station 45–60 minutes before departure. Allow more during holidays, when travelling with children or substantial luggage, or if you still need help with your booking. This is a planning buffer, not a claim that entering the station always takes an hour.

Shanghai’s municipal government confirms that the [Airport Link Line](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Transportation/20241231/f66f14bbd4b549ab88e6f3aec375790c.html) connects Pudong Airport T1 & T2 with Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2 in around 40 minutes. It is a different service from Metro Line 2.

## Before booking: three names to get right

| Name | Chinese | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station | 上海虹桥站 | The usual starting point for the direct high-speed route described here |
| Yiwu Railway Station | 义乌站 | Your intercity train destination |
| Yiwu International Trade City | 义乌国际商贸城 | The market complex; also commonly called Futian Market |

Shanghai Railway Station, Shanghai South and Shanghai Hongqiao are different stations. Other stations may have useful services, depending on where you are staying, but choose them deliberately. Do not accept an alternative simply because its name begins with “Shanghai.”

Likewise, “Yiwu Market” is not a precise final destination. Save the district, gate or hotel before departure.

## Booking the Shanghai–Yiwu train

### Where to buy

Use [China Railway’s official 12306 service](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=1), a railway ticket window or a reputable booking platform. Compare the train duration, seat availability and final payable price before confirming.

Third-party platforms may add service fees or offer their own cancellation and support arrangements. Read these separately from the railway’s rules.

For this route, search:

- From: Shanghai Hongqiao / 上海虹桥
- To: Yiwu / 义乌
- Your travel date and passenger details

Do not plan around a fixed timetable copied from a blog. Current listings include different stopping patterns, durations and fares. A cheaper or slower service from a different station may not suit an airport connection or a market visit.

### Foreign passports and identity verification

Book using the valid passport you will carry. Enter the passenger details carefully and bring the original document. An itinerary screenshot is useful for finding your train, but it does not replace your identification.

Complete any identity verification requested by 12306 before your travel day. The app may offer document-photo submission for review. If that does not work, contact support or visit a station ticket window with your passport. Follow the status and instructions shown in your account rather than assuming every passenger needs the same extra procedure.

Older traveller reports describe having to visit a station before online booking worked. Treat those as individual experiences, not a requirement applying to every foreign visitor.

### How much does the ticket cost?

There is no single route-wide fare. It depends on the service, seat class and applicable pricing. Check the current price for the exact direct train you intend to take, including any platform fee.

Budget separately for your Shanghai transfer and the final journey in Yiwu. A low headline train fare is not the whole door-to-door cost.

### Children’s tickets

For children travelling with adults, the age-based rules introduced on 1 January 2023 are stated in the [official 12306 FAQ](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=1):

- One child under six may travel free with each ticketed adult, without a separate seat.
- Children aged six to under fourteen need a child ticket.
- Passengers aged fourteen and above need a full-fare ticket.
- Additional children under six travelling with the same adult need the appropriate child ticket.

Include children in the booking as required and carry their travel documents. If you want a separate seat for a child who would otherwise travel free, check the booking options. Do not rely on the older height-based wording that remains in some guides.

### Changes, cancellations and missed trains

Check the conditions when booking. China Railway generally permits one ticket change, subject to available seats and the applicable rules. Charges can depend on when you change and whether you move to another travel date. Cancellation fees also increase closer to departure.

If you miss your train, contact the booking channel or station staff immediately. A change may still be available under the railway’s conditions; do not assume the ticket is automatically worthless or that you can simply board the next train. The [12306 after-sales guidance](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=1) sets out the details.

For an international flight connection, ticket flexibility is useful, but it is not a substitute for a realistic transfer buffer.

## Navigating Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station

### Allow time to find your way, not just to walk

Arriving at the metro station is not the same as reaching your high-speed train’s boarding gate. Follow signs to railway departures, pass the required checks and find your train number on the departure boards.

As a working estimate, allow around 10–20 minutes for walking and finding your way from the metro exit towards your gate, plus any security or identification queues. A familiar route with short queues may be quicker. A distant gate, heavy bags or a busy period can take longer.

That estimate is not a timed guarantee, and it is not a recommendation to arrive only 15–20 minutes before departure.

### Passport checks are usually brief; queues are less predictable

Keep your passport accessible at entry, boarding and arrival. If an automatic gate cannot read it, use the staffed lane or ask for help.

The check itself is usually straightforward. The less predictable part is waiting for staff or standing behind other passengers. We do not assign a fixed number of minutes to every checkpoint because conditions vary.

### Find the gate before getting food or coffee

Use your train number to locate the correct gate. Check the board again if you have been waiting for a while, and keep an eye on boarding announcements.

Ticket checking stops before the scheduled departure. The exact cutoff is governed by station arrangements. Arriving at the gate at the time printed as “departure” is too late.

### A sensible first-visit buffer

Aim to reach the railway station 45–60 minutes early on an ordinary day. A possible allocation is:

- Walking, orientation and required checks;
- Extra time for an unexpected queue or booking question;
- Time near the gate before boarding begins.

These are parts of one overall buffer, not extra allowances to add repeatedly. During major holiday travel, consider around 90 minutes or more if your circumstances warrant it.

## Pudong Airport to Yiwu: plan the connection properly

### Option 1: Airport Link Line, then the Yiwu train

The Airport Link Line makes the airport-to-airport section much faster than older guides suggest. Shanghai’s government describes an approximately 40-minute journey between Pudong T1 & T2 and Hongqiao Airport T2. A [November 2026 municipal update](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Latest-WhatsNew/20261104/718a3d2ae3b1440985dc1692d453c6e2.html) also confirms the service remains in operation.

The practical sequence is:

1. Clear immigration and collect checked bags at Pudong.
2. Follow signs to the Airport Link Line station serving T1 & T2.
3. Travel to Hongqiao Airport Terminal 2.
4. Follow the connection towards Hongqiao Railway Station departures.
5. Complete railway checks and board your booked Yiwu service.

The 40-minute figure covers the airport train ride—not landing-to-platform time, and not a guaranteed connection to the Yiwu train.

### Option 2: Metro Line 2

Metro Line 2 is a slower alternative. Compare its current journey and service hours with the Airport Link Line for your date and arrival time. Do not confuse the airport link, Metro Line 2 and the Maglev; they follow different routes.

A Maglev ride does not take you directly to Hongqiao Railway Station. If you choose it, account for the onward transfer.

### How long should you leave after an international flight?

For a first visit, we recommend at least four hours between the scheduled landing at Pudong and your high-speed train departure. This is a conservative planning allowance, not an official minimum connection time.

It includes uncertainties such as:

- Flight delay and getting off the aircraft;
- Immigration and baggage reclaim;
- Reaching and waiting for airport ground transport;
- The transfer at Hongqiao;
- Security, passport checks and finding the boarding gate.

A smooth connection can be quicker. A disrupted flight or long immigration queue can exceed this allowance. If missing the train would jeopardise an important appointment, arrive the day before or stay overnight in Shanghai.

Once you add the Yiwu train and final local transfer, roughly 6–7 hours from scheduled international landing to your Yiwu hotel or market is a conservative planning budget, not the fastest possible trip.

### What about a direct airport bus?

Third-party listings advertise a Pudong–Yiwu coach, but the available material does not establish a dependable current timetable. Do not make it your default without confirming the operator, departure date, pickup terminal, arrival location and ticket availability.

We therefore do not publish fixed bus departure times or promise a direct service.

## From Hongqiao Airport to the railway station

Check your arrival terminal before planning the walk.

- Terminal 2 is within the same transport hub as the railway station, but you still need to collect luggage and follow the departure signs.
- Terminal 1 is separate. Include the terminal transfer rather than assuming the Terminal 2 walk applies.

Domestic arrival formalities may be shorter than international ones, but baggage delays and unfamiliar directions still matter. Choose your train after considering the whole connection, not just the proximity of the buildings.

## Arriving at Yiwu Railway Station

Your booked destination is Yiwu / 义乌. On arrival, follow current signs for the station exit, urban rail, buses, official taxis or the designated app-based pickup zone.

An October 2026 local guide reports changes to Yiwu station’s building and interchange arrangements. We have not independently confirmed those layout details with a railway announcement. For that reason, this guide does not prescribe a gate number or an exact walk through the station.

### Choose the final transfer by luggage and destination

| Option | Published reference | Choose it when |
|---|---|---|
| Urban rail | A local guide describes four stops to International Trade City, around 12 minutes on the train and about RMB 5 | You have light luggage and the destination stop suits your hotel or market entrance |
| Official taxi or app-based car | Allow roughly 25–40 minutes to the Trade City area in normal traffic; longer if busy | You have suitcases, companions or a precise hotel/gate to reach |
| Bus 801 | Published guidance suggests roughly 30–45 minutes, depending on the stop and road traffic | You want a budget daytime option and have checked the current service |

The local rail and bus details above are from [easyiwu’s station-to-market guide](https://easyiwu.com/knowledge/yiwu-railway-station-to-futian-market), not a live operator timetable. The rail ride time excludes reaching the platform, waiting and walking out at the market end. Verify directions, service hours and fares before relying on the connection.

### Do not tell the driver only “Yiwu Market”

International Trade City covers multiple districts. Ask your supplier or hotel for a Chinese location pin and the exact district, gate, building or street address.

The local guide places the urban-rail arrival on the District 1 side. If your destination is elsewhere in the complex, check the last stretch rather than assuming you can walk straight into the right building. A direct taxi may save handling luggage twice.

Our [Yiwu Market Map and District Guide](/guides/yiwu-market-map/) explains how to choose the right part of the market.

### Taxis, ride-hailing and late arrivals

Use the signed official taxi rank or the pickup area specified in your ride-hailing app. For taxis, use the meter; for app-based rides, confirm the quoted estimate and that a driver has accepted the booking.

Do not confuse a submitted request with a confirmed car. Match the registration and pickup point before boarding. Avoid unsolicited transport offers from people approaching you inside the station.

Check the current fare estimate for your date, district and vehicle; road traffic can affect the final journey time and metered fare.

If arriving late, check that the urban rail or bus is still running. Do not rely on an old screenshot of the last service. Have a taxi or hotel pickup alternative and tell your hotel if you expect a late check-in.

## Hotel, luggage and your first market visit

### Choose accommodation around your actual destination

For a market-focused trip, choose a hotel based on the district where you expect to work. “Near Futian Market” can still leave you on the wrong side of the complex.

Before booking, check:

- The exact location relative to your preferred market entrance;
- Reception and late-arrival arrangements;
- Whether luggage can be stored before check-in or after checkout;
- Whether breakfast and early departure suit your plans;
- Vehicle pickup access if you will meet suppliers elsewhere.

A station-area hotel is not automatically the most convenient base for several market days.

### Store luggage before a serious buying session

Going straight to the market can make sense if you arrive early, have light bags and know where to start. Otherwise, store luggage first.

Dragging a suitcase through several floors reduces your time for comparing products, recording booth numbers and handling samples. Ask your hotel about storage; do not assume a stall or the station will hold your bags.

### Is an afternoon arrival worth a market visit?

Possibly—for a short orientation, an agreed pickup or a narrowly focused visit. It is not automatically too late, but do not treat a late afternoon as a full buying session.

For example, with a 14:00 train arrival, the platform exit, transfer, luggage drop and journey to the correct entrance may use much of the remaining afternoon. A 12-minute urban-rail ride does not mean you are at a supplier’s booth at 14:12.

Check that the supplier will still be available. Building opening hours and individual stall hours are not identical.

For an important meeting, the following morning is the safer default—particularly after an international flight.

## Can you visit Yiwu as a day trip from Shanghai?

Yes, but choose a narrow goal. A day trip is more realistic for collecting specific samples, revisiting known suppliers or exploring one product category than for finding and comparing an entire new supply chain.

A workable structure is:

1. Take an early direct train after allowing time to reach Hongqiao.
2. Transfer to a chosen district or meeting point.
3. Concentrate on a small supplier list or one category.
4. Keep a break for food and organising notes.
5. Leave for the station with time for road delays, security and boarding.

For the return leg, a cautious starting budget is around 1.5–2 hours between leaving the market and your train departure when using a car. This combines a road transfer, possible waiting and a station buffer; it is not an official requirement or a measured average. Increase it in poor weather, heavy traffic, holidays or if you need to collect luggage first.

Confirm the return ticket and avoid using the final possible service as your only plan. You do not need to stay until the market closes to make a day trip worthwhile.

## Luggage and shopping samples on the train

China Railway’s [published FAQ](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=1) lists a normal adult free carry-on allowance of 20 kg, with individual EMU-train items limited to 130 cm in combined dimensions and 20 kg. Check current instructions if carrying unusually large or heavy samples.

Keep passports, booking details, essential medication and valuables accessible. Put larger bags where permitted, without obstructing aisles or doors, and keep anything you cannot lift or manage yourself out of the plan.

Bottled alcohol, aerosols, batteries and commercial samples may be restricted. Check the railway’s current restricted-items instructions. Clear original labels help staff identify the contents, but sealed packaging alone does not establish that an item is allowed.

You do not need to worry that every normal personal item will cause a problem. The useful step is to check unusual samples before buying them—not at the security checkpoint on your return journey.

## A realistic time budget

These are planning allowances for first-time visitors, not guaranteed minimum journey times.

| Scenario | Suggested planning approach |
|---|---|
| Central Shanghai hotel to Yiwu hotel or market | Budget roughly 3.5–5 hours, then adjust for your hotel location and chosen train |
| Arriving at Pudong on an international flight | Leave at least four hours from scheduled landing to the train; roughly 6–7 hours to the Yiwu destination with this buffer |
| Arriving at Hongqiao Airport | Check Terminal 1 versus Terminal 2, baggage collection and the station connection |
| First visit to Hongqiao Railway Station | Arrive 45–60 minutes before departure; consider 90 minutes during major holiday travel |
| Yiwu train arrival to an important meeting | Prefer the following morning; otherwise agree a generous, flexible arrival window |
| Leaving the market for a return train | Start with a 1.5–2-hour transfer-and-station budget; add luggage collection or disruption time |
| Yiwu back to Shanghai for an international flight | Work backwards from the airline’s check-in requirements; consider returning to Shanghai the previous day |

Do not add every buffer twice. For example, if your four-hour Pudong connection already includes railway-station time, you do not need to add another hour on top by default. Review the full sequence once and identify where delays could occur.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a direct Shanghai-to-Yiwu train?

Yes. Direct services operate from Shanghai Hongqiao. Compare the departure station, duration and availability for your travel date before booking.

### How long does the train take?

Many direct services take roughly 1.5–2 hours, with the fastest advertised journeys around 1 hour 20 minutes. Slower services take longer. The duration on your selected train is more useful than a route-wide average.

### How far is Shanghai from Yiwu?

The distance depends on the starting point, destination and rail or road route being measured. For practical planning, use your selected train’s duration or a current road route rather than treating conflicting city-to-city mileage figures as interchangeable.

### Can I get to Yiwu directly from Pudong Airport?

The practical rail route is airport ground transport to Hongqiao, followed by the intercity train. The Airport Link Line takes about 40 minutes to Hongqiao Airport T2, but the terminal-to-railway transfer and railway procedures are additional. Confirm any advertised direct coach before depending on it.

### Do I have to visit a station to verify my passport before booking?

Not necessarily. Follow 12306’s current account-verification instructions and complete any requested review early. If online verification fails, use support or a station ticket window. Do not turn one traveller’s past experience into a universal rule.

### Can children under six travel free?

One child under six can travel free without a separate seat with each ticketed adult under the age-based arrangements stated by 12306. Include the child as required and carry identification. Separate seats and additional children require appropriate booking arrangements.

### Is the market right beside Yiwu Railway Station?

No. Plan a local transfer and identify the exact district or hotel. Even after getting off at International Trade City’s urban-rail station, you may need another walk or ride.

### Should I take a taxi or urban rail in Yiwu?

Urban rail is useful with light luggage when its stop suits your destination. A taxi or app-based car is simpler for an exact hotel or gate, substantial bags, children or late arrivals. Confirm current local services before choosing.

### Do I need a Chinese phone number?

Booking requirements depend on the service. Have working mobile data, an accessible booking confirmation and a way to contact your hotel. Set up any payment and ride-hailing apps before arrival where possible, and save key addresses offline.
